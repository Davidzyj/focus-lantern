English

Focus Lantern helps you start focused work sessions, play gentle procedural ambient sounds, and review your daily focus statistics.

Common Questions

  • Where is my data stored? Focus records are stored locally on your device.
  • Do I need an account? No account is required.
  • Why do the sounds not play? Check your device volume and silent mode, then tap the speaker control in the app.
