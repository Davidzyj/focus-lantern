Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: May 26, 2026

English 中文 Support / 支持

English

Focus Lantern is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Data Stored on Device

The app may store your focus session history, selected timer mode, selected ambient sound, and related statistics using local device storage. This data stays on your device unless you choose to remove the app or clear device data.

Ambient Sounds

Focus Lantern uses procedural audio generation for built-in ambient sounds. Audio is generated on the device and does not require uploading recordings or personal data.

Network and Analytics

The app does not require an account, does not use third-party analytics, and does not send your focus history to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Focus Lantern does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us through the support page.

中文

专注灯设计为在你的设备本地运行。我们不会收集、出售、出租或分享个人信息。

设备本地存储

应用可能会使用设备本地存储保存你的专注记录、计时模式、环境音选择和相关统计。这些数据保留在你的设备上，除非你主动删除应用或清除设备数据。

环境音

专注灯内置环境音由程序在设备上实时生成，不需要上传录音或个人数据。

网络与分析

应用不需要账号，不使用第三方分析服务，也不会把你的专注历史发送到我们的服务器。

儿童隐私

专注灯不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请通过支持页面联系我们。
